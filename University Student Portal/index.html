🏛️ University Vision & Campus Facilities

The University of Layyah (UL) provides modern undergraduate and graduate programs taught by foreign and local PhD/MPhil qualified faculty to empower students with practical skills.

Core Campus Facilities (Unordered List):

  • Smart Computing Labs: High-speed PERN fiber internet and modern workstations.
  • Central Digital Library: Free student access to IEEE, Springer, and HEC digital books.
  • Accessible Ground-Floor Halls: Dedicated ramp access and ground-floor rooms for disabled students.
  • Student Facilitation Center: One-window support for ID cards, fee challans, and scholarships.

📋 Online Course Registration Guide

Follow these four sequential steps to complete your semester course enrollment on the University of Layyah Student Portal:

Enrollment Procedure (Ordered List):

  1. Review the Weekly Class Schedule Table below to check teacher names and timings.
  2. Browse or search available courses in the Courses Section (or add a new course).
  3. Complete all required fields in the Student Registration Form.
  4. Check the Admin Applied Students Dashboard below to view your registered profile.

❓ Student Frequently Asked Questions

Important academic, faculty, scholarship, and portal answers for UL students.

University of Layyah has 160+ highly qualified faculty members, including foreign and local PhD Doctors, HEC-Approved Research Supervisors, and Gold Medalist MS/MPhil scholars from top institutions (UK, NUST, COMSATS, UAF, and PU).

Deserving and high-achieving students can apply for the Chief Minister Honhaar Merit Scholarship, Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF), HEC Need-Based Scholarship, and kinship fee concessions.

Minimum 75% class attendance is mandatory to sit in final semester exams. For students who select Disability: Yes during registration, all lectures and labs are arranged on the Ground Floor (GF).
